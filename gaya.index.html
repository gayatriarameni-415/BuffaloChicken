Buffalo Chicken Caesar Salad

Caesar salad is a simple dish made with romaine lettuce, croutons, and Parmesan cheese, mixed with Caesar dressing. The dressing is creamy and usually includes garlic, lemon, and sometimes anchovy for flavor.

Ingredients and Materials:

  • 1 head of Romaine lettuce (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup Croutons
  • 10 pc Wingstop Buffalo Boneless Wings
  • 1/4-1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/3 cup Caesar dressing (store-bought or homemade)
  • Large bowl
  • Knife
  • Cutting board
  • Tongs or spoon
  • Plate or serving bowl

Instructions:

  1. Prep the lettuce: Wash, dry, and chop lettuce. put it in a large bowl.
  2. Add basic ingredients: shake the lettuce with Caesar sauce until its mostly coated. Add croutons and Parmesan.
  3. Prep the chicken: If the wings cold, warm the wings quicly in the oven/microwave/air fryer
  4. Avengers Assemble: Put the buffalo wings on top of the salad. You can leave 'em whole or cut 'em into smaller pieces.
  5. Finish up: Add extra Parmesan, a squeeze of lemon, and a drizzle of ranch or blue cheese if you feel like it.
  6. eat
